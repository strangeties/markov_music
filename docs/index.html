"In C" by Terry Riley is a composition consisting of 53 musical phrases played by independent voices. Voices begin together but gradually diverge: at the end of each phrase, a voice has the option to repeat the previous phrase or play the next one. For more information, please visit https://teropa.info/blog/2017/01/23/terry-rileys-in-c.html.

This is a synth rendition accompanied by a visualization of the Markov chain traversed by voices.

When performed live, players respond not only to unique internal probabilites but also to the external environment, including each other (all to say this is a simplification of the myriad interactions available to a human ensemble!).
